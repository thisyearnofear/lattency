# Patron notice & capture-day ops copy (v0.1)

**Status:** draft — signage wording and "is notice sufficient" questions need counsel for Germany/France/Kenya (see `docs/venue-3d-research.md` open questions). UK pilot wording below.

---

## Entrance notice (printed card, placed at the door during capture)

> **3D scanning in progress**
>
> This venue is being filmed to create a 3D model for Lattency, a workspace map.
> A Lattency contributor is filming the room between **__:__ and __:__**.
>
> Faces and personal items are blurred automatically before the model is made.
> The raw video is deleted within 72 hours.
>
> **Don't want to appear?** Tell the person filming and we'll stop, or ask the
> venue staff. You can also ask later: **lattency.app/report-scan** — we'll
> blur you or remove the scene.
>
> Questions: hello@lattency.app

*(Keep it A6 postcard size — big enough to be real, small enough not to be theatre. The "tell us and we'll stop" line matters more than legal prose.)*

---

## Capture-day checklist (contributor-facing, in-app before filming)

1. Confirm the venue's signed consent is on file (in-app: "venue has opted in" state).
2. Place the entrance notice where people walk in.
3. Film during the agreed off-peak window only — a near-empty room makes a better model and has fewer faces to blur.
4. Walk a slow loop covering every public area. Don't film restrooms, staff-only areas, or anything the venue flagged.
5. If anyone asks you to stop — stop. Tell them about the blur/removal option.
6. No audio needed — we strip it on upload anyway.
7. Upload within 24 hours. Raw footage is deleted from our side within 72 hours.

> Saw yourself in a venue scan, or want a scan removed?
> Tell us the venue and roughly when you visited — we'll blur you or take the
> model down. Venue owners can claim and manage their scan here too.

## Known gaps for counsel

- Whether posted notice alone satisfies patron consent in Germany/France (likely needs opt-out to be genuinely easy — hence the stop + blur-later remedies).
- Kenya: notice may need to name the data controller and contact per DPA s.29 transparency duties.
- Whether "blur your face" requests need identity verification (probably a light-touch confirmation, not an ID check).
